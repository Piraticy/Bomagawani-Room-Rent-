A Piece of Paradise on the Indian Ocean
The villa sits on a dreamlike stretch of the Indian Ocean: white sandy beach, mangrove-lined
coastline, and crystal-clear waters with a fascinating underwater world.
Here you experience every day:
 spectacular sunrises over the Indian Ocean
 tropical nature and impressive biodiversity
 absolute tranquility away from the hectic European everyday life
The region is among the most pristine and beautiful coastal areas of East Africa.
Afro-Arabian Architecture
The house was built in the Afro-Arabian style, combining traditional architecture of the
Swahili coast with functional design for the tropical climate.
A characteristic feature of this style is the generous wrap-around veranda that surrounds
the house. Its solid construction expands usable living space and creates shaded outdoor
areas.
Architectural highlights:
 robust and solid construction
 ceiling height of about 3.5 m, ensuring comfortable temperatures even in heat
 natural ventilation through barred window openings
 expansive wrap-around veranda with views of nature and the ocean
The property has a very solid structural base; only a few finishing works on the bathrooms
and the rainwater tank are still planned.
Good accessibility – and yet absolute quiet
The port city of Tanga is only about 30 km away and offers good infrastructure:
 markets with fresh fish, vegetables, and local produce
 banks, doctors, and shopping
 airport and port
